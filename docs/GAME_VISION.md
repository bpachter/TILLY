# TILLY Game Vision

## Fantasy

Run a fragile exploration vessel where homey daily rituals exist side-by-side with incomprehensible cosmic danger.

## Narrative Arc (v1)

1. Earth departure and crew formation.
2. Mars Colony handoff and refit.
3. Outer-system expedition chain:
   - Europa
   - Enceladus
   - Ganymede
   - Titan
   - Ceres
   - Pluto
4. Discovery phase: anomalous biosignatures and non-human intelligence.
5. Escalation phase: alien and AI-hybrid threats.
6. Endgame choices: preserve humanity, merge intelligence, or retreat with partial truth.

## Player Loop

1. Plan jump route and resources.
2. Handle ship incidents and social events.
3. Resolve tactical encounters.
4. Scan and recover scientific samples.
5. Return to calm mode: repairs, farming, cooking, relationship events.
6. Upgrade ship and crew capabilities.

## Emotional Target

- 60% wonder and attachment.
- 25% tension and dread.
- 15% emergency panic.

## Differentiators

1. Cozy systems materially impact tactical outcomes.
2. Personality-rich crew creates emergent stories each run.
3. Exploration in the Solar System with plausible science framing.
4. Distinct factions: alien, rogue AI, and hybrid entities.
